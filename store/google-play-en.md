## Full description (max 4000)
CG Trivia is the daily general-knowledge game that trains your mind in just 2 minutes a day.

<b>A new question every day</b>
The same question for everyone. Answer, keep your streak and climb the daily, weekly and global rankings.

<b>Eight ways to play</b>
• Daily question — one shared question a day, compare with friends.
• Time Attack — how many can you answer in 30 seconds?
• Climb Mode — rising difficulty, lives and a pot you risk or cash out.
• Challenges: Flags — 196 countries, by continent or across the whole world.
• Challenges: Years — 98 historical events. Can you pin each one to its year?
• Learn — pick a topic and practice; we explain the context when you miss.
• Adventure — travel through themed chapters, face guardians and collect relics.
• Play with Friends — pass the phone, scoreboard, 1v1 duels, survivor and trivia night.

<b>Learn while you play</b>
2,000 free questions across 13 categories: history, science, art, geography, philosophy, sports, biology, film, music, literature, technology, mythology and astronomy. Every wrong answer comes with an explanation.

<b>Optional CG PRO</b>
Expand the catalogue to 4,000 questions, open all 400 Adventure levels across 20 chapters, and add Smart Review, Exam and advanced insights. Prices and terms are shown before you confirm a purchase.

<b>Progress that hooks you</b>
Level up, earn coins, complete daily missions, unlock achievements and titles, and compete in weekly leagues across divisions.

<b>Made your way</b>
Light theme, dark theme or whatever your system uses.

Free to download and play. CG PRO is an optional purchase. Available in English and Spanish — pick your language in Settings.
