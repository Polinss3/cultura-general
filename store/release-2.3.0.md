# Versión 2.3.0 — textos preparados para App Store Connect

Estos textos describen las novedades respecto a la 2.2.0 publicada. No enviar
a App Review hasta instalar y comprobar la compilación definitiva en TestFlight.
La 2.2.0 ya anunció los 400 niveles, CG PRO, Examen y Repaso inteligente;
no presentarlos de nuevo como funciones nuevas.

## Novedades (Español, España)

La pregunta diaria estrena una pantalla de preparación: lee las instrucciones
y empieza cuando estés listo. Las ligas semanales reúnen a jugadores en grupos
de unos 30. También puedes recuperar una racha perdida con monedas durante un
plazo limitado.

Hemos reorganizado la tienda en potenciadores y cosméticos, añadido nuevos
estilos PRO y mejorado su aspecto en la app. Tus temas favoritos aparecen más
a menudo en Aleatorio y Contrarreloj. La interfaz se adapta mejor al iPad y
hemos pulido diversos detalles visuales y de estabilidad.

## What's New (English)

The daily question now has a preparation screen: read the short instructions
and start when you're ready. Weekly leagues bring players together in groups
of around 30. You can also recover a lost streak with coins for a limited time.

The shop now separates power-ups from cosmetics, with new PRO styles and
improved visuals throughout the app. Your favourite topics appear more often
in Random and Time Attack. We've also improved the iPad layout and polished
visual details and stability.

## App Review Information → Notes (English)

VERSION 2.3.0 — replace BUILD NUMBER with the build selected for review.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 2.2.0
The daily question presents a preparation screen before showing the question
or its answers. Tap Continue to start; the response timer starts then. The
shop separates power-ups and cosmetic items. Weekly leagues use groups of
about 30. A lost daily streak can be recovered with coins for a limited
period. We also added cosmetic styles, weighted favourite topics in Random
and Time Attack, and improved the iPad interface.

HOW TO REVIEW
An account is optional for Adventure, Time Attack, Climb Mode, Flags, Years
and Learn. Use the demo account supplied in the login fields to review the
daily question, leagues and friend rankings. To see the preparation screen,
open the Daily tab before answering that day's question. Tap Continue to
start. Open Shop to see the Power-ups and Cosmetics sections.

PURCHASES AND PRIVACY
CG PRO remains optional. The monthly and annual subscriptions and lifetime
purchase are unchanged from 2.2.0; TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox.
The app does not track users across other companies' apps or websites, does
not use IDFA, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. The
App Privacy information no longer includes Device ID. The in-app account
deletion path is Profile → Danger zone → Delete account.

## Antes de enviarla a revisión

- Sustituir `BUILD NUMBER` por la compilación efectivamente probada.
- Comprobar en TestFlight apertura, pregunta diaria, tienda, compra y restauración,
  liga, iPad y notificaciones push en un dispositivo físico.
- Comprobar que App Privacy publicada no indica datos usados para seguimiento.
- Confirmar qué funciones de notificación están activas en producción antes de
  mencionarlas en las notas públicas.
